# Q3 LATAM Market Entry Plan

## Executive Summary

Launch SaaS Sales Cloud in Mexico City, São Paulo, and Bogotá this quarter.
Pricing: localized USD list minus 30% for new logos, 12-month duration.
Headcount: 6 hires (2 AE, 2 SE, 1 CSM, 1 RevOps). Target $1.2M ARR Q3,
$3.5M Q4. TAM: $2.4B LATAM SMB segment.

## Market Analysis

Mexico City, São Paulo, and Bogotá represent ~62% of LATAM digital-native
SMB CRM spend. Competitors (Pipedrive, HubSpot) sell through resellers
with no dedicated AE coverage in these three metros — Salesforce covers
São Paulo enterprise only. We will differentiate on: native Portuguese +
Spanish UI, local-currency billing, in-region AE presence.

## Pricing & Packaging

Keep the existing 3-tier US plan (Starter, Pro, Enterprise). Localize via
a 30% discount off USD list for new LATAM logos in their first 12 months,
billed in local currency (MXN, BRL, COP) via Stripe. No new LATAM-only SKU.

## GTM Plan

Hiring (complete by Q3 week 4):
  - Mexico City: 1 AE, 1 SE
  - São Paulo: 1 AE, 1 SE
  - Bogotá: 1 CSM (covers all three metros)
  - Remote: 1 RevOps Lead (Mexico City TZ)

Channel: direct field sales + referral pilot with VAR-A (Mexico + Bogotá)
and VAR-B (São Paulo + Rio). No paid acquisition in Q3.

Launch: phased — Mexico City week 4, São Paulo week 7, Bogotá week 10.

## Risks

  1. **FX exposure** on MXN, BRL, COP — Treasury baseline hedges 50% of
     forecast revenue, reassess quarterly.
  2. **Hiring slippage** — LATAM AE cycle averages 90 days; backup
     candidates pre-screened for the Mexico City AE role.
  3. **Localization quality** — Spanish UI in QA, expected sign-off week 1;
     Portuguese UI sign-off week 2. CRO has authority to defer a market
     launch if localization isn't ready.

## Financial Projections

  - Q3 ARR target: $1.2M (60% MX, 25% BR, 15% CO)
  - Q4 ARR target: $3.5M (assumes all launches on time + US AE-ramp baseline)
  - Headcount cost: $720K annualized
  - Marketing & ops: $180K Q3 (events + local PR)
  - Total Q3 investment: ~$405K (within $450K guardrail)

## Timeline

  - Week 1-2: localization sign-off, VAR contracts.
  - Week 3: team onboarded, sales playbook adapted.
  - Week 4: Mexico City launch event.
  - Week 7: São Paulo launch.
  - Week 10: Bogotá launch + Q3 reforecast checkpoint.
