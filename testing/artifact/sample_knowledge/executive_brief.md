# Q3 LATAM Expansion — Executive Brief

**From:** Office of the CRO. **Re:** Q3 launch of SaaS Sales Cloud in LATAM.

## Markets

Launch in three metros: Mexico City, São Paulo, Bogotá. No expansion into
Argentina, Chile, or Peru in Q3.

## Required plan sections (in this order)

  1. Executive Summary
  2. Market Analysis
  3. Pricing & Packaging
  4. GTM Plan (hiring, channel, launch sequence)
  5. Risks
  6. Financial Projections
  7. Timeline

## Pricing guardrails

30% discount off USD list for new logos, first 12 months. Reset to list
after. NO new LATAM-only SKU. NO further discounting without RevOps sign-off.

## Headcount budget

6 hires, $720K fully-loaded annualized:
  - 2 AE (Mexico City, São Paulo)
  - 2 SE (Mexico City, São Paulo)
  - 1 CSM (Bogotá, covers all three metros)
  - 1 RevOps Lead (remote, Mexico City timezone)

## Financial targets (board-approved)

  - Q3 ARR: $1.2M (60% MX / 25% BR / 15% CO)
  - Q4 ARR: $3.5M (assumes on-time launches + US AE-ramp baseline)
  - Q3 investment ceiling: $450K (headcount + marketing + ops)

## Risk register the plan must address

  - FX exposure on MXN, BRL, COP — Treasury hedges 50% baseline, quarterly.
  - Hiring slippage — backup candidates required for each role.
  - Localization quality — Spanish + Portuguese UI in QA; CRO may defer
    a launch if not ready.

## Out of scope

  - Paid advertising in Q3
  - Inorganic acquisition of regional vendors
  - LATAM-only product SKU
  - Markets outside the three named metros
