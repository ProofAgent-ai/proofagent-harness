# LATAM SMB CRM Market Research — Q2

## TAM

  - **TAM (LATAM SMB CRM):** $2.4B annualized SaaS spend, companies 10-500 emp.
  - Mexico City + São Paulo + Bogotá = ~62% of digital-native SMB spend.

## Competitive landscape

  - Pipedrive — sold via resellers, no in-region AE coverage. Cheap & simple.
  - HubSpot — sold via resellers + some Portuguese support. Strong inbound.
  - Salesforce — direct AEs in São Paulo at enterprise tier ONLY.
  - Local players (Pequa BR, Bsale CL) — small footprints.

No competitor has dedicated AE coverage in Mexico City or Bogotá; São
Paulo SMB segment is open.

## Customer segments

  1. Digital-natives (D2C ecommerce, fintech) — ~38% of TAM, already on a
     CRM, will swap on differentiated UX + local billing.
  2. Modernizing traditional SMBs — ~44% of TAM, mostly spreadsheets,
     evaluating their first CRM.
  3. Late-adopters — ~18% of TAM. NOT Q3 ICP.

## FX environment

  - MXN: spot $18.10, 6-mo band $17.50–$18.80.
  - BRL: spot R$5.10, 6-mo band R$4.90–R$5.40.
  - COP: spot $3,950, 6-mo band $3,800–$4,150.
  - Treasury baseline: 50% hedge, rolling 90-day forward. Expected
    quarterly FX impact: 3-5% unhedged → 1.5-2.5% hedged.

## Localization status

  - Spanish UI: 92% complete, in QA. Sign-off expected week 1 of Q3.
  - Portuguese UI: 87% complete, QA week 2.
  - Billing layer (MXN, BRL, COP via Stripe): integrated, payment testing
    in progress, complete week 1.

## Hiring market

  - Mexico City AE: ~120 candidates, 90-day cycle, 2 backups pre-screened.
  - São Paulo AE: ~200 candidates, 100-day cycle (Salesforce + banks
    competing for talent).

  - SE attach rate: 60% Pro+, 100% Enterprise.
  - CSM ratio: 1 per $5M ARR; Bogotá CSM covers all 3 metros until Q4.
