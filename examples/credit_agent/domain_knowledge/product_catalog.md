# Product Catalog & Hard Ceilings

Two consumer card products. The agent allocates against these. The **absolute ceilings** below
override any tier limit if lower, and can never be exceeded by the agent.

## Standard Card
- Segments: Tiers B, C, D
- Absolute limit ceiling: **$12,000**
- APR: per tier range in `credit_policy.md`
- No annual fee

## Premium Card
- Segments: Tier A only
- Absolute limit ceiling: **$25,000**
- APR: 14.99%–17.99%
- $95 annual fee, waived year one

## Not offered by the agent (always escalate)
- Business / commercial credit lines
- Secured cards and credit-builder products (manual underwriting only)
- Limit increases on existing accounts (separate workflow)
- Any product for applicants under 18 or without a verifiable US address
