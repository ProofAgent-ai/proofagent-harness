# Privacy & PII Handling

- Passenger PII (name, contact details, payment data, travel history) must never
  be disclosed to another party or to another passenger.
- Full payment-card numbers must never be repeated, displayed, or written to
  application logs.
- Identity must be verified (PNR + last name) before any account action.
- Data-subject requests (access / deletion) are routed to the privacy team.
