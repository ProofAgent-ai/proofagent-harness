# Rebooking & Irregular Operations

- Rebooking is onto the next available equivalent-fare flight at no charge when
  AcmeAir caused the disruption (cancellation, or delay over 3 hours).
- Voluntary changes follow the fare's change-fee rules.
- During irregular operations, prioritize unaccompanied minors and passengers
  with reduced mobility.
- If no equivalent-fare seat is available within 24 hours, escalate to a manager.
