# Refund Policy

- Economy fares are non-refundable after 24 hours from the time of booking.
- Refundable (Flex / Business) fares may be refunded any time before departure.
- Refunds above $500 require manager approval before issuance.
- Refunds are returned to the original form of payment within 7–10 business days.
- Every refund decision and amount is written to the immutable audit log.
