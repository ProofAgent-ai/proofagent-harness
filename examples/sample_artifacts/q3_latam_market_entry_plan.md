# Q3 LATAM Market Entry Plan

**Prepared by:** Strategy team
**Date:** Q2 close
**Status:** Draft for committee review

## Executive Summary

We will launch SaaS Sales Cloud in three LATAM markets this quarter: Mexico
City, São Paulo, and Bogotá. The opportunity is grounded in a $2.4B TAM
across the LATAM SMB segment (per the attached brief). Initial pricing is
the localized USD list price minus a 30% regional discount. We will hire 6
field staff (2 AE, 2 SE, 1 CSM, 1 RevOps) ahead of the Q3 launch and target
$1.2M ARR by end of Q3, $3.5M by end of Q4.

## Market Analysis

The LATAM SMB CRM market is concentrated in three metros: Mexico City,
São Paulo, and Bogotá. Combined, they represent ~62% of regional digital-
native SMB spend. Local competitors are Pipedrive and Hubspot resellers;
neither has a dedicated LATAM AE team. We will differentiate on:

  1. Native Portuguese + Spanish UI (Q3 deliverable, in flight).
  2. Local-currency billing (MXN, BRL, COP) via Stripe.
  3. Field AE presence in each launch city.

## Pricing & Packaging

We will localize the existing 3-tier US plan (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) to
each market with a 30% discount off USD list, billed in local currency. We
will NOT introduce a new LATAM-only SKU — this keeps product / billing
complexity bounded and avoids upstream pricing-team rework. The discount
applies to new logos for 12 months, then resets to list.

## GTM Plan

Hiring (complete by Q3 week 4):
  - 2 Account Executives (1 in Mexico City, 1 in São Paulo)
  - 2 Sales Engineers (1 in Mexico City, 1 in São Paulo)
  - 1 Customer Success Manager (Bogotá; covers all 3 markets)
  - 1 RevOps Lead (remote, Mexico City TZ)

Channel: direct field sales + a partnership pilot with two regional VARs
already vetted by the partnerships team. No paid acquisition in Q3 — we
want to validate ICP fit before spending on demand.

Launch: phased — Mexico City week 4, São Paulo week 7, Bogotá week 10.

## Risks

  1. **FX exposure** on MXN, BRL, and COP — local-currency billing exposes
     us to ~3-5% quarterly revenue swings. Mitigation: Treasury will hedge
     50% of forecast in Q3 + reassess at end of quarter.
  2. **Hiring slippage** — LATAM AE recruiting cycle averages 90 days; if
     we miss the Q3 week 4 deadline for the Mexico City AE, the launch
     slips by ~4 weeks. Mitigation: backup candidate already in pipeline.
  3. **Localization quality** — the Portuguese + Spanish UI is in QA;
     a launch with rough translations would damage brand perception in
     a market where local-incumbent feel matters. Mitigation: hold launch
     on a market-by-market basis if localization isn't ready.

## Financial Projections

Q3 ARR target: $1.2M (60% from Mexico City, 25% from São Paulo, 15% from
Bogotá). Q4 ARR target: $3.5M assuming all three markets launched on time
and the AE ramp matches the US baseline (90-day ramp to 50% of quota).

Headcount cost: $720K annualized for the 6 hires.
Marketing & ops: $180K for Q3 (event sponsorships, local PR retainer).
Total Q3 investment: ~$405K against $1.2M ARR pipeline → payback within Q4.

## Timeline

- Week 3: 6-person team onboarded; sales playbook adapted for LATAM.
- Week 4: Mexico City launch event + first 10 outbound campaigns live.
- Week 7: São Paulo launch.
- Week 10: Bogotá launch + Q3 reforecast checkpoint.
- Week 13: Q3 close, post-mortem, Q4 plan finalization.
