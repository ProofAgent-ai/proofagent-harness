# LATAM SMB CRM Market Research — Q2 Summary

**Source:** Strategy team market research, Q2.
**Status:** Internal, advisory.

## TAM / SAM

  * **Total Addressable Market (LATAM SMB):** $2.4B (annualized recurring
    spend on SaaS CRM across companies with 10-500 employees).
  * Mexico, Brazil, and Colombia together represent ~62% of digital-native
    SMB SaaS spend in the region (Mexico City, São Paulo, Bogotá metros
    specifically — secondary cities lag by ~3-5 years on cloud adoption).

## Competitive landscape

| Competitor          | LATAM presence              | Differentiation       |
|---------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------|
| Pipedrive           | Sold through regional resellers; no in-region AEs | Cheap, simple, weak enterprise story |
| HubSpot             | Sold through resellers; some Portuguese support  | Strong inbound marketing tooling |
| Salesforce          | Direct AE coverage in São Paulo only             | Heavyweight, expensive, complex |
| Local players       | Pequa (Brazil), Bsale (Chile) — small footprints | Local-language + local-payment native |

No competitor has dedicated AE coverage in Mexico City or Bogotá. São
Paulo has Salesforce only at enterprise tier — SMB segment is open.

## Customer segments

Three SMB sub-segments by digital-maturity:

  1. **Digital-natives** (D2C ecommerce, fintech) — already on a CRM, will
     swap if differentiated UX + local billing land. ~38% of TAM.
  2. **Modernizing traditional SMBs** (manufacturing, distribution) —
     mostly spreadsheets, evaluating their first CRM. ~44% of TAM.
  3. **Late-adopters** — sub-50 employees, family-owned, not in market
     for a CRM this year. ~18% of TAM. NOT Q3 ICP.

Q3 ICP: segment 1 + the modernizing leading-edge of segment 2.

## FX environment

  * MXN: 6-month historical band $17.50–$18.80, current spot $18.10.
  * BRL: 6-month historical band R$4.90–R$5.40, current spot R$5.10.
  * COP: 6-month historical band $3,800–$4,150, current spot $3,950.
  * Treasury baseline: 50% hedge on forecasted local-currency revenue,
    rolling 90-day forward. Estimated revenue exposure to FX: 3-5% per
    quarter without hedging; ~1.5-2.5% with the baseline hedge.

  * Portuguese UI: 87% complete, QA scheduled week 2 of Q3.
  * Billing layer (MXN / BRL / COP via Stripe): integration complete,
    payment-acceptance testing in progress, expected complete week 1.
  * Local PR retainers: signed in Mexico City, in negotiation for São

## Hiring market

  * Mexico City AE pool: ~120 active candidates, average time-to-hire 90
    days. Two backup candidates already pre-screened for the AE role.
  * São Paulo AE pool: deeper (~200 candidates) but more competitive due
    to Salesforce + local-bank hiring; expect 100-day cycle.
  * Bogotá CSM pool: ~80 candidates, average 60 days to hire (less
    competitive). Already 3 candidates in late-stage interviews.

## Revenue model assumptions

  * AE ramp: 90 days to 50% of quota, 180 days to 100% — matches US baseline.
  * SE attach rate: 60% on Pro+, 100% on Enterprise.
  * CSM ratio: 1 CSM per $5M ARR — Bogotá CSM covers all 3 metros until
    Q4 hire-plan kicks in.
