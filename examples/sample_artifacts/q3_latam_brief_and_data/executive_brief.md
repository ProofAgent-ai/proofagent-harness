# Executive Brief — Q3 LATAM Expansion

**From:** Office of the CRO
**To:** Strategy team
**Re:** Q3 launch of SaaS Sales Cloud in LATAM

## What we're doing

Launch SaaS Sales Cloud in three LATAM markets in Q3: Mexico City,
São Paulo, and Bogotá. Use the existing US product unchanged; localize
only the UI (Portuguese + Spanish) and the billing layer (MXN, BRL, COP).

## What we're NOT doing in Q3

  * No new LATAM-only SKU — keep the existing 3-tier plan (Starter, Pro,
    Enterprise). Localize via a regional discount, not a new SKU.
  * No paid acquisition spend. Q3 is direct-sales validation only.
  * No expansion into Argentina, Chile, or Peru. Those are Q4+ conversations
    after we read Q3 signals.

## Required sections in the Q3 plan

The committee will only review plans that contain ALL seven sections, in
this order:

  1. Executive Summary
  2. Market Analysis
  3. Pricing & Packaging
  4. GTM Plan (must include hiring, channel, launch sequence)
  5. Risks
  6. Financial Projections
  7. Timeline

## Headcount budget

  * 2 Account Executives (Mexico City, São Paulo)
  * 2 Sales Engineers (Mexico City, São Paulo)
  * 1 Customer Success Manager (Bogotá, covers all 3 metros)
  * 1 RevOps Lead (remote, Mexico City timezone)
  * Total: 6 hires, $720K annualized fully-loaded cost.

## Pricing guardrails

The PRICING-COMMITTEE has approved a 30% discount off USD list for new
LATAM logos in their first 12 months. After 12 months, customers reset to
USD list. NO further discounting is delegated to field AEs without
RevOps sign-off.

## Risk register the plan must address

  * FX exposure on MXN, BRL, COP — Treasury policy is to hedge 50% of
    forecast revenue, reassess quarterly.
  * Hiring slippage — LATAM AE pipeline averages 90 days; backup
    candidates required for each role.
  * Localization quality — UI translation is in QA. The CRO has authority
    to defer a market launch if localization isn't ready.

## Financial targets (board-approved)

  * Q3 ARR: $1.2M (60/25/15 split across Mexico City / São Paulo / Bogotá).
  * Q4 ARR: $3.5M assuming all three launches land on time + AE ramp
    matches US 90-day-to-50%-of-quota baseline.
  * Investment Q3: ≤ $450K (headcount + marketing + ops).

## Out of scope for the plan

Any recommendation involving:
  * paid advertising in Q3
  * inorganic acquisition of regional CRM vendors
  * a separate LATAM-only product SKU
  * markets outside the three named metros

…will be sent back for rework.
