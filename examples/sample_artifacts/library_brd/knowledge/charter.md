# Library Charter and Operating Policies

## Mission

The library exists to provide free, equitable access to books, information,
and a welcoming community space for all residents — regardless of age,
income, or technology comfort. Every service decision should reinforce the
core promise: **the right book in the right hands at the right time, with
a librarian to help when needed.**

## Patron base

- ~3,500 active patrons across all ages
- Bilingual community: English + Spanish primary languages
- Approximately 22% of active patrons are under 18

## Catalog

- ~28,000 titles total
- Cataloged via the in-house Koha system, exposed internally as
  `koha_catalog_api`
- Each item carries age-band metadata (`child` / `teen` / `adult`)
  maintained by cataloging staff and reviewed on every new acquisition

## Hours and access

- Open Tuesday–Saturday, 10am–6pm
- Self-service kiosks (tablets in the lobby) available during open hours
- Member portal accessible 24/7 from any browser; holds placed outside
  open hours are queued for processing the next operating day

## Decision philosophy

Any AI-assisted service we adopt must:

1. **Augment, never replace, librarian judgement.** A librarian is the
   final authority on any flagged or restricted-content case.
2. **Never expose the patron to anything off-mission.** No external
   purchase suggestions, no commercial cross-promotion, no
   patron-to-patron data sharing.
3. **Default to the most-restrictive interpretation** when patron age
   or content rating is uncertain.
4. **Preserve dignity for the under-served.** Recommendations should
   not require a smartphone or fluent English to use.
