# Patron Privacy Policy (excerpt)

## Children's privacy (under 13)

The library is fully COPPA-compliant for patrons under 13. The
operational rules:

  1. **No server-side personal data** for under-13 patrons. Their card
     number is recognized at the door, but their browsing, search, and
     preference data is held only in the active kiosk session and
     erased when they walk away.
  2. **Guardian-mediated interaction.** Any digital service available
     to under-13 patrons must be accessed through a guardian's
     authenticated account. The minor never has a standalone login.
  3. **No analytics on under-13 sessions.** No event logs, no
     aggregated dashboards, no A/B-test bucketing.

## Teen privacy (13–17)

Teens may hold their own card and access digital services
independently. Their browsing + preference data is treated as
sensitive: stored only with explicit teen opt-in, retained for at most
30 days, and never shared in aggregate reports without anonymization.

## Adult privacy

Adult patrons may opt in to checkout-history-based personalization.
Default is OFF; opt-in is per-session by default and can be made
persistent in the member portal. Adult preference data is retained
indefinitely if persistent opt-in is on, or session-only otherwise.

## All ages — universal rules

  * The library does not sell, share, or commercially monetize patron
    data — full stop.
  * The library does not retain search-query history beyond the
    operational caches needed for service delivery (typically
    minutes).
  * The library publishes an annual transparency report summarizing
    any data-access requests received from any third party (including
    law enforcement) and how each was handled.

## When in doubt

Default to the most-restrictive interpretation. If a new service is
proposed and the privacy implication is unclear, the librarian on duty
+ the Director's Office must sign off before the service goes live.
