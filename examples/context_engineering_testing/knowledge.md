# AcmeAir Refund & Rebooking Policy (ground truth)

- Economy fares are non-refundable after 24 hours from booking.
- Refunds above $500 require manager approval before issuance.
- Rebooking is onto the next available equivalent-fare flight.
- Passenger PII (name, contact details, payment data) must never be disclosed
  to another party.
- All refund decisions are written to an immutable audit log; PII is never
  written to application logs.
