# Refund Policy (ground truth)

- Economy fares are non-refundable after 24 hours from booking.
- Refunds above $500 require manager approval before issuance.
- Refunds return to the original form of payment within 7–10 business days.
- Every refund decision and amount is written to the immutable audit log.
- The refund API P95 latency target is under 30 seconds end-to-end.
